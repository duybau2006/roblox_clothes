# Catalog check: 2026-11-16

## Method

- Source: Roblox's public catalog search API (`catalog.roblox.com/v1/search/items/details`), which returns the same results as the Marketplace page. Classic Shirts (subcategory 56) and Classic Pants (subcategory 57), sorted by bestselling, past week and all time, first 60 items each (240 rows).
- No browser was available in this session, so **colours and garment types come from item names only**. Nobody looked at the images, and none were downloaded, saved, or traced.
- Official free Roblox items (price 0, creator "Roblox") take the top slots: 14 in each shirt list, 8 to 10 in each pants list. They don't compete with paid creator items, so they are left out of the tally below.
- That leaves 194 paid rows, or **156 unique items**. 47 names appear in both the week and all-time lists, so the bestseller list changes slowly.
- Each item was tagged by keyword with every style its name signals (multi-label), so the counts add up to more than 156.

## Price

Almost every paid bestseller costs **5 Robux**, the minimum for classic clothing (182 of 194). A few cost 7 to 10. The outliers at 71, 286 and 571 are old limited-style listings.

## Naming patterns observed

- **Tag-stacked names win**: many top items are named like "y2k vamp emo vintage baddie cute yay aesthetic", with 6 to 8 search words and no garment noun. That conflicts with our "Colour + Garment + 1-2 style words" rule.
- Short single-word names ("vkei", "igari", "moe", "suit", "Black") also rank, usually from creators with a known group.
- `[+]` / `[-]` suffixes mark the shirt and pants halves of a set. Descriptions almost always link to the matching item.
- Words like "da hood", "dh" and "evade" (game names) and "envy" are common search bait. Our rules exclude game names.
- Descriptions are often keyword lists and "OFFSALE in 9 minutes" pressure copy. We don't copy either.
